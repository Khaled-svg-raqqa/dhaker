She also sees it as her duty to build their characters. “No to violence” is a motto in her classes. Mrs. Al-Hroub plans to establish her own foundation and use some of her winnings to train other teachers to follow her approach. It is worth mentioning that Mrs. Al-Hroub's dream was to be a doctor but failing to do so did not prevent her from being a special person who could make a difference.
